# Relatable

The viewer recognises themselves before they know it is about a product.

- **Hook:** "POV:" or "me when", in the second person or as a caption to a
  feeling. "POV: you swiped the reminder away and felt productive."
- **Beats:** the joke escalates, one short line per slide.
- **Product:** the real screen as the punchline, with a line that stays in
  the joke's voice.
- **End:** the product name.

Good for: reach and comments. Weakest of the six for installs, so judge it
on profile visits, not views. No health or diagnosis framing, even as a
joke, unless the brief opts in.
