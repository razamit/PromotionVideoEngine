# Things that fixed [problem]

A list people save. The hook names one problem the viewer has; each slide
after it is one thing that helped, numbered.

- **Hook:** "3 things that fixed my [problem]". A number, the problem in the
  viewer's own words, nothing about the product.
- **Numbers:** give each thing its place in the list with `number: 1`, not
  in the line. It is drawn large above the line. The count in the hook must
  match. A second product slide that only adds a detail has no number.
- **Tips:** real habits or tricks that work without the product. They must be
  true and useful on their own, or the list reads as an ad in disguise. Never
  another app's screen, and never a named competitor.
- **Product:** tip 2 or 3, never tip 1. Say what it does in the viewer's
  words, over a crop of the real screen that shows it. A second product slide
  may show one detail (a setting, the result). The name may appear here.
- **End:** the product name.

Good for: saves, reach. The product is one of several answers, which is what
keeps it from looking like an ad.
